Ceramic coating spotlight

Protection with real presence.

Three coating options, applied to carefully prepared paint. Choose the one that fits how you drive and how long you plan to keep your vehicle.

3-Year Ceramic Coating

Gloss, hydrophobic properties and easier maintenance. A dependable way to protect and enhance your paint.

7-Year ArtDeShine Pro Graphene

Our long-term option, using ArtDeShine Pro Graphene ceramic coating for a deep, lasting gloss.

FEYNLAB Ceramic Coating

Professional FEYNLAB ceramic coating, available for customers who prefer this brand.

  • Enhanced gloss

    A deeper, richer shine that makes paint look its best.

  • Hydrophobic properties

    Water tends to bead and roll off the surface instead of clinging to it.

  • Easier maintenance

    A slicker surface helps everyday washing go more quickly and simply.

  • Environmental protection

    A protective layer that helps shield paint from everyday environmental exposure.

Results and longevity depend on the vehicle, its preparation, and how it is cared for. We will talk you through what to expect when we confirm your appointment.
